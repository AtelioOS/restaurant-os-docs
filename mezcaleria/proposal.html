What it actually does

The things you said were falling through

  • The receipts nobody hands in. Invoices are captured and coded as they arrive, so the month does not end with you chasing paper. The accountant gets a clean export instead of a shoebox.
  • Labour against what the room is actually doing. Rostering built from forecast covers rather than last week's roster copied forward — which is the argument for whether lunch comes back, and at what staffing.
  • The cellar. Around two hundred agave labels counted in bottles, pour cost on every drink, and the bottles open longest surfaced instead of buried at the back.
  • Catering as a real product. Patio as the entry tier, Mezcalería as the elevated one, each with structured packages and prices so quoting an event is not a negotiation from scratch every time.
  • The phone answered, every time. An AI host that picks up when the room is full — hours, directions, the booking handed to Tock, and a catering enquiry written down instead of lost. It never sends anyone to voicemail at seven on a Friday.
  • Marketing in one place. Google profiles, reviews and replies, campaigns running to a ceiling you set — on your own card, never through us.
  • A training system that sticks. Your manual, loaded in, assigned, and tracked — so implementing it is not another thing that needs you standing over it.

Private, and yours

Your instance is built for you and is not shared with any other business. Nothing you put in it trains anything that anyone else uses, and nothing about your operation leaves it. Your data is exportable in full, at any time, and there is no lock-in period — you can stop whenever you want.

Catering and handover. Patio and Mezcalería packages priced, event calendar and client records live, Google corrected, your team trained, and us out of the room.

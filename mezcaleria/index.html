Documents to gather

Two of these gate a specific week — they are marked

What we will never ask you for

Online banking credentials. Not now, not later, not for any reason. We do not connect to your bank, we do not use an aggregator, and we hold no standing access to your money — you export statements, the platform reconciles them. If anyone ever asks you for a banking login on our behalf, it is not us. This is written into clause 6 of your agreement and it is not negotiable from our side either.

Decisions only you can make

We can start without these, but each one blocks a specific sprint

How should catering report?

Patio and Mezcalería events run as their own reporting lines alongside the restaurant, each with its own revenue and margin. Tell us if you want them cut differently.

Your catering packages and prices

Patio as the entry tier — tacos, burritos, quesadillas. Mezcalería as the elevated one — tequila and mezcal service, cuts, seafood. We need the package list and what each should sell for.

How do the catering tiers split?

Patio as the entry tier — tacos, burritos, quesadillas for corporate and casual. Mezcalería as the elevated tier — tequila and mezcal service, cuts, seafood. We need the package list and prices for each.

Who sees finance, and at what level?

Per person, not per role. You and the bookkeeper are assumed; tell us about anyone else.

Monthly marketing ceiling, per venue

Meta and Google bill your own card directly — that money never passes through us. You set the cap.

Which three businesses share the monthly?

The $1,200 is one figure for the whole arrangement, invoiced across your businesses in whatever proportions you set, so each one deducts its own share. We need the names and the percentages.

Which venue goes live first?

We would suggest Commercial Drive, as the fullest operation. But you know where the pain is.

The eight weeks

Each sprint is done when the evidence exists in your own system — not when we say so

Week 0

Readiness. Every item above granted and tested. Your system switched on — your brand, the restaurant and both catering lines, fifteen modules on.

Done when: every credential has been used once to pull real data.
Weeks 1–2

The restaurant live. La Mezcalería configured with its three reporting lines, Toast and Tock connected, delivery platforms in, sales landing the same day.

Done when: yesterday's sales and covers are in the platform by 9am, with nobody touching a spreadsheet.
Weeks 3–4

The cellar. Around two hundred agave labels counted in bottles, pour cost on every drink, menus costed against your real invoices, and the bottles open longest surfaced instead of buried.

Done when: you can open any cocktail and see its true pour cost, its margin, and which bottle moved.
Weeks 5–6

The money. Bank, card and QuickBooks exports reconciled, a true P&L for the restaurant and for each catering line, payroll and scheduling moved off their current tools, rostering against forecast covers.

Done when: you have a P&L per venue you recognise, and labour against sales is visible day by day.
Weeks 7–8

Catering and handover. Patio and Mezcalería packages structured with real prices, the event calendar and client records live, Google profiles claimed and corrected, reviews in one place, training for your team in English, and us stepping out of the room.

Done when: you can quote and invoice an event end to end, and La Mezcalería runs a full week with no intervention from us.
